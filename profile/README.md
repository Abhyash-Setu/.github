## Who We Are

**Abhyash Setu** is a Nepal-based technology company focused on education and AI.

*Setu* means **bridge**. We build products that connect students with quality practice, fair evaluation, and smart guidance.

---

## What We Do

| Area | Description |
|---|---|
| EdTech Products | Learning and exam-practice platforms for Nepali students |
| Applied AI | AI systems for grading, feedback, and learning support |
| Software Engineering | Modern, scalable web products built for Nepal |

---

## Our Products

### Abhyasalaya

Our flagship EdTech platform for Nepali students.

- **For:** CA / ICAN, NEB +2, and university students
- **AI Answer Grading:** rubric-based marking of student answers
- **Mock Tests and Chapter-wise Practice**
- **Performance Analytics** for students
- **Teacher and Admin Tools**

> More products coming soon.

## Contributing

1. Create a branch from `dev`
2. Name it `feature/short-name`
3. Commit with clear messages
4. Open a Pull Request and wait for 1 review
5. Merge after approval

Never commit secrets, API keys, datasets, or model weights.
